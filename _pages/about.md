I am Bilgehan, a PhD student in AI safety and security at [CWI](https://www.cwi.nl/en/groups/computer-security/) Amsterdam, advised by Prof. [Marten van Dijk](https://www.cwi.nl/en/people/marten-van-dijk). I work towards safe, secure, and private AI that remains practically useful, with guarantees that hold both in theory and in practice.

My current work focuses on the safety of LLM post-training and agentic systems. Together with Prof. [Srini Devadas](https://people.csail.mit.edu/devadas/) (MIT), I work on certified defenses against data poisoning in post-training and on robust communication and collaboration in multi-agent systems. I also study privacy in machine learning, from the [fundamental limits of DP-SGD](https://doi.org/10.1145/3830454.3832629) (ACM CCS 2026) to [PAC-private fine-tuning of LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06505) (NeurIPS 2026).
